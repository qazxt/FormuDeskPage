2. Formula Content and Calculation History

According to the app's stated design, formulas you enter or edit, calculation inputs and results, and formula history are processed and stored locally on your device. This information is not intentionally uploaded to a developer-operated server.

The developer does not operate a server-side formula history service according to the information provided for this policy.

If you choose to copy, export, share, or otherwise transfer content to another app or service, the selected content may be handled by that destination under its own terms and privacy practices.

FormuDesk's OCR processing is described as taking place locally on your device. Images or recognized formula content are not intentionally sent to the developer for OCR processing.

Before publication, this statement must be checked against the actual implementation, including any OCR framework, optional feature, or network-dependent fallback used by the released app.
